# Arbor Company Research Brief

Date: 2026-09-05
Audience: job candidate / company evaluator
Scope: Find Arbor Incorporated / Arbor at findarbor.com, not similarly named energy or network-security companies.

## Executive Answer

Arbor is a seed-stage New York enterprise AI company building Umi, an AI researcher/voice-interview platform for frontline and customer conversations. The product pitch is that enterprises in transportation, manufacturing, logistics, retail, hospitality, and customer experience lose valuable qualitative signal in offline/frontline conversations, and Arbor turns those conversations into executive-ready themes, quotes, dashboards, and action recommendations.

## Evidence Notes

- Official site describes Arbor as an "AI-powered embedded research team" that hears from the frontline and turns conversations into actionable intelligence; the platform flow is Create, Listen, Understand, Act.
- Official site FAQ says Umi runs natural voice-based interviews with frontline employees and customers at scale, can support anonymous conversations, identifies operational issues/root causes/emerging risks across roles/locations/shifts, and produces synthesized themes, quotes, recommendations, reports, and dashboards.
- Official about page lists leadership as Veronica Ma, CEO & Co-Founder; Kelly Zhou, COO & Co-Founder; Ashish Dsa, CTO & Co-Founder.
- Official security page claims SOC 2 Type 2, encryption in transit and at rest, 24/7 monitoring, GDPR/CCPA compliance, SBOM monitoring, third-party VAPT, AES-256 at rest and TLS/SSL in transit.
- PRNewswire release, sourced to Find Arbor Incorporated and dated Feb. 3, 2026, says Arbor raised $6.3M in combined seed/pre-seed funding. Seed led by 645 Ventures, with Next Play Ventures, Chaac Ventures, Comma Capital, angels, and an earlier unannounced pre-seed. It says funds will support product development and New York team expansion.
- PRNewswire release names First Student and Lyons Magnus as customers and says early customers are achieving 85-90% participation rates and identifying seven-figure cost savings. These are company-sourced performance claims.
- 645 Ventures portfolio page confirms active seed investment in 2025 and describes Arbor as an AI operations consultant using unstructured operational data including conversations, paper workflows, and siloed systems. It says Arbor integrates with platforms like Workday and ERP systems and positions against Workday/Qualtrics as systems of record.
- 645 Ventures portfolio page lists founders as Kelly Zhou, Veronica Ma, and Sam Dorison, conflicting with Arbor's official site and the PRNewswire release naming Ashish Dsa as CTO/co-founder. Treat official site and PRNewswire as stronger for current leadership, and 645 as evidence of either outdated/inaccurate metadata or a prior founder/role history.
- LinkedIn page lists Arbor as privately held software development company in New York, 2-10 employees, and "view all 14 employees"; Built In says HQ New York, 16 employees, founded 2025. Dealroom and registry-derived pages suggest launch or incorporation around 2022. Treat employee/founding dates as inconsistent across third-party sources.
- Built In job page for Founding GenAI Engineer lists stack/use cases: real-time voice agents with LiveKit, Pipecat, Twilio, Deepgram, ElevenLabs; agentic LLM workflows on OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini; Python/FastAPI; TypeScript/React; pipelines for themes, quotes, recommendations; production observability, evals, guardrails. It lists compensation $150K-$220K, senior level, in-office New York City.
- Careers page emphasizes small NYC team, in-person heartbeat, early-career or seasoned applicants, fast shipping, research/product/customer proximity, equity, health benefits, flexible time off, learning budget, offsites/customer travel, parental leave.
- Terms of Service last updated Apr. 22, 2025 says customer owns Customer Content and derived data from Customer Content, while Arbor may collect/analyze usage/performance data to improve services. It assigns responsibility to the customer to ensure shared data was collected fairly, legally, and with employee consent.
- Competitive set/inference: Arbor overlaps with AI-moderated research platforms such as Outset, Listen Labs, Conveo, Versive, and broader CX/EX/research tooling like Qualtrics, Medallia, Culture Amp, UserTesting/Maze, plus consulting/research agencies. Arbor differentiates by focusing on enterprise operations/frontline workforces, voice interviews, and action-oriented operational intelligence rather than general consumer research alone.

## Limitations

- No private customer contracts, retention, revenue, valuation, SOC 2 report, or product demo were accessible.
- Customer ROI, participation, and "large enterprise" traction claims are mostly company-sourced, not independently audited in public.
- Founder, employee count, and founding-year metadata conflict across sources.
